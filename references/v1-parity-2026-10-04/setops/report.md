## summary (2026-10-04)

no MISSING, no V2_REGRESSION, no UNDOCUMENTED_DIFFERENCE found in this slice. every v1 result in the slice is reachable in v2,
either directly or by a composition that was RUN and reproduces v1's correct results. v1 defects found by the probes
(all fixed in v2): `issubset(number)` compares through float; `union/add(x)` on an empty self and `difference/discard/remove`
of a single number crash (AttributeError); `difference()` raises; multi-operand `symmetric_difference` with an even number of
others is wrong (k=0 returns {}); `∅ in ∅` / `∅.issuperset(∅)` False; ±inf arguments and `expand(inf)` hit AssertionErrors.
doc hazard (not a gap): the §4 surface map's pointer "`relations.overlaps/adjoins`" for `or_adjacent=` is only right per piece;
`A.adjoins(B)` compares the outer cuts of whole sets and misses adjacency to an inner piece of B. the gap-filling
composition that replaces `merge_adjacent(distance=)` is written nowhere in v2's docs.
